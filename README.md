## Presentation Excerpt: "A Letter To Employers..."
"Thank you for the time and effort that you and your organization invested throughout the interview process. I truly appreciate the opportunity to meet with the team and participate in the numerous interviews, the one-hour presentation, and screening discussions.
After careful consideration, I have decided to move forward with another organization whose opportunity aligns more closely with my current career goals and personal needs.
I understand how competitive your selection process is, and with that in mind, I regret to inform you that your organization was not selected. While this may come as a surprise, I hope you can appreciate the irony after the thorough evaluation process we both experienced.
I will certainly keep your organization in mind for future opportunities and would not hesitate to recommend you to colleagues should the right situation arise.
Thank you again for your time, professionalism, and consideration. I wish you and your team continued success and the very best in finding the right candidate for the role."

## Original Abstract
Abstract/Objective:
This presentation provides an in-depth review of today’s cybersecurity hiring landscape, examining what organizations are truly looking for in candidates across skills, experience, education, certifications, and professional presence. It explores how evolving business demands, cloud adoption, compliance requirements, and emerging technologies such as AI are reshaping workforce expectations and recruitment strategies. The presentation also addresses the significant challenges candidates face during the hiring process, including Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), AI-driven resume filtering, recruiter ghosting, third-party recruiters, ineffective resumes, LinkedIn optimization, and the disconnect between job postings and actual hiring needs. Through industry analysis, market trends, and practical guidance, this presentation delivers insight into how cybersecurity professionals can better position themselves for success in an increasingly competitive and automated hiring environment.
